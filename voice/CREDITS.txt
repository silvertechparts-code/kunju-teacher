Kunju Teacher: recorded teacher voices (Malayalam, English, Hindi)

The sentences in this folder (pack-0.js, pack-1.js, ...) were spoken by computer voices:

MALAYALAM (a female voice)

Changes made by us: each Malayalam sentence was spoken clause by clause (a short pause was put at every comma), silence at the
start and end was cut, the loudness was made equal for all sentences, and the audio was compressed to mono MP3 (24 kHz, 40 kbit/s) and stored as base64 text inside the .js files, so the page also works when index.html is opened as a file.
A few Malayalam sentences with English letters or digits were written out in Malayalam letters before speaking
(for example "A" as "ഏ"), so that the voice says them as a teacher would. English and Hindi sentences were spoken at 0.95 speed.

You may use and share these recordings, even in a commercial product, as long as you keep this credit
(the app shows a short form of the same line in the Grown-ups page, under "Voice source").
