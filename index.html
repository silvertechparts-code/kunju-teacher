Privacy

Everything stays on this device: progress, uploaded text and the questions your child asks. The teacher answers from her own notes inside this page, and nothing is sent to an AI service. The app never saves recordings. To turn speech into words, the browser itself may send the voice to its own speech service (for example Google in Chrome); that is a browser feature, not part of this app. Children are never asked for names, addresses or phone numbers.
